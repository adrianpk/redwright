# RedWright Brand Art Direction v1

## Status
Normative style direction.

This document defines how RedWright should look and feel visually, beyond structural logo rules.

Reference image:
- `docs/reference/ui/assets/logo-reference.png`

---

## Visual North Star
RedWright shall read as:
- artisan engineering,
- trustworthy craft,
- industrial precision with human character.

It shall not read as:
- cartoon mascot brand,
- neon tech startup,
- sterile enterprise default.

---

## Pseudo Vintage Signature
The style uses a modernized vintage workshop language.

Key traits:
1. Cream paper-like background tone.
2. Deep navy linework with clear contour hierarchy.
3. Warm red accents used on focal elements only.
4. Slight print-like texture/distress, never noisy.
5. Serif-driven wordmark presence with heritage rhythm.

---

## Main Character Direction
## Character Role
The character is a craft operator of the software factory, not a mascot.

## Character Shape Language
- Clean silhouette and upright posture.
- Distinct cap and tool silhouette.
- Confident but neutral facial expression.
- Proportions that remain legible at medium and small display sizes.

## Linework
- Primary outlines in navy with clear stroke dominance.
- Interior details lighter than outer contour.
- Avoid over-detailed micro-lines that collapse in UI contexts.

## Color Placement
- Cap and focal accents in red family.
- Garment and contour structure in navy family.
- Skin/neutral zones remain understated and subordinate to brand colors.

---

## Wordmark Direction
## Tone
- Assertive serif typography.
- High contrast between `RED` and `WRIGHT` blocks.
- Letter spacing and stroke weight must preserve readability first.

## Styling
- Texture may suggest print/craft heritage.
- Distress effects must be subtle and controlled.
- Decorative separators must remain secondary.

---

## Material and Texture Rules
Allowed:
- soft grain,
- light print-wear hint,
- mild edge roughness in brand marks.

Not allowed:
- heavy noise overlays,
- glitch effects,
- photoreal filters,
- gradients that flatten navy/red contrast.

---

## Composition Rhythm
The full mark composition should preserve:
1. Character block as top anchor.
2. Wordmark block as dominant center weight.
3. Signature line as grounding footer.
4. Symmetry and centered balance in lockups.

Any variant should keep this reading order unless an explicit alternative lockup is specified.

---

## UI Translation Rules
1. Operational surfaces:
- keep branding restrained (palette + typography cues),
- avoid placing full character lockup in dense Kanban workspace.

2. Identity surfaces (login, onboarding, docs):
- full logo lockup is encouraged,
- character can be prominent.

3. Favicon:
- use simplified monogram mark only,
- never force full character in favicon context.

---

## Do and Do Not
Do:
- keep cream/navy/red balance,
- keep character confident and technical,
- keep composition centered and breathable.

Do not:
- make character comedic,
- over-saturate red across large surfaces,
- replace serif identity with generic geometric sans in primary logo contexts,
- over-animate brand marks in operational UI.

---

## Review Checklist
Before accepting any brand-facing change, verify:
1. Does it still read as artisan-industrial?
2. Are navy and red used with restraint and hierarchy?
3. Is the character still legible and non-cartoonish?
4. Is texture subtle rather than loud?
5. Does the result match the reference mood in `logo-reference.png`?

## Illustration Blueprint
This section is intentionally explicit so an image generation system can infer the target output with low ambiguity.

## Subject
- A vintage craft engineer from chest-up to mid-torso.
- Adult male-presenting figure with calm, focused expression.
- Facing slightly to the viewer's right (three-quarter pose).
- Holding a wrench near chest level with one hand.

## Wardrobe and Props
- Red flat cap (textured fabric look).
- Work overalls with visible straps and metal buckles.
- Light shirt under overalls.
- Classic wrench in red tone family.

## Line and Rendering Style
- Dominant navy ink-style outlines.
- Secondary inner strokes for facial features and garment folds.
- Slight print-distress texture on fills and strokes.
- Clean silhouette, no painterly brushwork.

## Composition
- Character centered.
- Wordmark centered below character.
- Decorative separators and small emblem beneath wordmark.
- Overall lockup vertically balanced with clear breathing room.

## Wordmark Treatment
- Large serif display lettering.
- `RED` in red family.
- `WRIGHT` in navy family.
- Subline in uppercase serif, lower contrast than main wordmark.

## Background
- Flat cream tone (`#F4EFE6`) with very subtle paper grain.
- No scenery, no environmental objects.

## Palette Constraints
- Primary navy: `#1F2A44`
- Secondary navy accents: `#3A4A63`
- Primary red: `#B23A2F`
- Deep red accents: `#8C2C24`
- Cream background: `#F4EFE6`

## Negative Constraints
- No cartoon proportions.
- No exaggerated smile or comedic expression.
- No neon colors or cyberpunk glow.
- No glossy 3D effects.
- No photoreal skin rendering.
- No modern minimalist sans-only wordmark replacement.

## Output Intent
- Must read as an artisan software factory identity.
- Must feel handcrafted but precise.
- Must remain legible as a full lockup and simplifiable to monogram forms for favicon usage.
